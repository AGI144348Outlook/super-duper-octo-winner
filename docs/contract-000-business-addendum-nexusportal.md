## Business hypotheses requiring evaluation

A user-controlled AI screen companion may offer useful cross-app guidance. A free core with a rolling two-feature premium window may support a sustainable product. Provider interoperability, market demand, Android platform feasibility, cost and monetization are **unverified**.

## Existing infrastructure

A Vercel project `nexusportals-diagram` exists; its READY deployment is not equivalent to a confirmed public NexusPortal website. Inspect before any modification. Cloudflare, Render and Neon are candidate infrastructure, not assumed live dependencies.
