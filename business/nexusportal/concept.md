# NexusPortal business concept

## User problem

AI assistants are typically isolated from what users are doing in other apps. NexusPortal proposes a user-invoked companion layer that can observe only with permission and return visual guidance over the user's screen.

## Experience

The floating `%` bubble opens connection settings. The user selects a supported AI and independently authorizes screen observation and annotation. The transparent layer draws highlights, arrows, circles, text and symbols without intercepting touches; the user can collapse, pause or stop the session. Launching an AI app is not proof of an authenticated MCP connection.

## Validation risks

Android MediaProjection and overlay restrictions; provider-specific integration; tenant isolation and revocation; privacy; app-store compliance; accessibility; sustainable free-tier infrastructure.

## Evidence status

Concept only. No verified live native AI-app connection, production gateway, market validation or public launch is claimed.
