## Request an agreement

Start a licensing inquiry through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/AGI144348Outlook/super-duper-octo-winner/issues)
with the subject `Commercial licensing inquiry`.

In your inquiry, describe the intended product or service, what project
components you wish to use, the intended distribution and business model,
and a way to arrange a private licensing conversation. Do not post
confidential business plans, personal contact information, or financial
details in a public issue.

A negotiation or an inquiry does not grant permission. Until a separately
signed agreement is in place, the repository's noncommercial license is the
only permission supplied by the project rights holders.

## Third-party components

Dependencies and code under separate third-party licenses retain those
licenses. This project cannot sublicense rights it does not own. Parties
seeking commercial terms should separately examine dependency compliance.

This page is an invitation to negotiate, not an offer of fixed commercial
terms. For substantial commercial projects, have qualified counsel review
the final agreement.
